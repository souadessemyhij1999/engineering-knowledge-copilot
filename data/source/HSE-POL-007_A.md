## 1 Purpose

This policy describes how we protect employees from noise. It follows the EU noise directive 2003/10/EC and its German implementation (LärmVibrationsArbSchV).

## 2 Exposure values

| Value | Daily exposure LEX,8h |
|---|---|
| Lower exposure action value | 80 dB(A) |
| Upper exposure action value | 85 dB(A) |
| Exposure limit value (with hearing protection) | 87 dB(A) |

## 3 Measures

- Above 80 dB(A): hearing protection is provided and employees are informed and trained. Occupational health check-ups are offered.
- Above 85 dB(A): the area is marked as a noise area with the blue sign and wearing hearing protection is mandatory. Occupational health check-ups are mandatory. A noise reduction programme must be set up.
- The exposure at the ear, taking hearing protection into account, must never exceed 87 dB(A).

## 4 Noise areas in the plant

| Area | Measured LEX,8h |
|---|---|
| Hall 3 machining (MC-500) | 84 dB(A) |
| Compressor room | 91 dB(A) |
| EOL test noise booth (outside) | 78 dB(A) |
| Press shop hall 1 | 88 dB(A) |

## 5 Hearing protection

Hearing protection is available at the dispensers at the hall entrances. Personal moulded ear plugs are offered to employees who work in noise areas for more than 2 hours per day.
