## 1 Participants

Plant manager, heads of production, quality, maintenance, logistics, HR and controlling.

## 2 Business figures

Q3 output was 41,850 e-drive units, 3 % below plan because of the supplier problem with housing castings. Cost of poor quality in Q3: 312,000 EUR (Q2: 405,000 EUR).

## 3 Decisions

1. A second source for housing castings will be qualified. Budget approved: 180,000 EUR for tooling. Target: PPAP approval by 2027-03-31.
2. The interlock for the spindle warm-up on all MC-500 machines is approved (see 8D-2026-021). Budget: 24,000 EUR.
3. Hiring freeze for indirect functions until the end of 2026, except for two quality engineers.
4. Line L3 for the new ED-260 variant: start of production postponed from 2027-02 to 2027-05.

## 4 Risks

The customer will reduce call-offs for ED-150 by about 15 % in Q1 2027. Short-time work in machining is being discussed with the works council.

## 5 Next review

The next management review takes place on 2027-01-18.
