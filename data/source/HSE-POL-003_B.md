## 1 Purpose

This policy describes how hazardous substances such as sealants, adhesives, coolants, cleaning agents and oils are stored, used and disposed of in the plant.

## 2 Safety data sheets

A current safety data sheet (SDS) must be available for every substance before it is used. SDSs are available in the HSE portal and in the yellow folder at each line. Workplace instructions (Betriebsanweisungen) based on the SDS are posted at every workplace where the substance is used.

## 3 Personal protective equipment

| Substance | PPE |
|---|---|
| Anaerobic sealant S-2 | nitrile gloves, safety glasses |
| Cleaning agent C-1 | nitrile gloves, safety glasses, good ventilation |
| Coolant concentrate | nitrile gloves, safety glasses, apron when mixing |
| Hydraulic oil | nitrile gloves |

## 4 Storage

Flammable liquids are stored only in the hazardous materials store or in approved safety cabinets. At the workplace, at most the quantity needed for one shift is allowed. Containers must always be labelled. Never fill substances into food or drink containers.

## 5 Spills

Use the spill kit at the line. Small spills: absorb with binder, put the binder in the red waste container. Large spills or spills into drains: call the internal emergency number 112 from a plant phone.

## 6 Skin protection

Coolant can cause skin disease. Use the skin protection plan at the washing area: protective cream before work, cleaning, and care cream after work.
