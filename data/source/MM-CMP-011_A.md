## 1 Scope

This manual covers the two screw compressors K1 and K2 (each 75 kW), the refrigeration dryer and the compressed air network of the plant.

## 2 Operating values

| Value | Target |
|---|---|
| Network pressure | 6.5 bar (alarm below 6.0 bar) |
| Pressure dew point after dryer | +3 degrees C |
| Oil carry-over | max 0.01 mg/m3 |

## 3 Maintenance plan

| Task | Interval |
|---|---|
| Check condensate drains | daily |
| Replace air intake filter | every 2000 operating hours |
| Replace oil separator cartridge | every 4000 operating hours |
| Oil change compressor | every 8000 operating hours |
| Leak detection round with ultrasonic detector | every 3 months |

## 4 Leak management

Leaks are the biggest energy loss in the compressed air system. During the leak detection round, every leak is marked with a yellow tag and entered in the maintenance system with location and estimated size. Leaks are classified:

- Class A (audible, more than 10 l/min): repair within 1 week
- Class B (3 to 10 l/min): repair within 4 weeks
- Class C (below 3 l/min): repair at the next planned stop

A leak with a diameter of 3 mm at 6.5 bar loses roughly 10 l/s of air, which is about 3 kW of compressor power.
