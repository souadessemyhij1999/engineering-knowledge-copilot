## 1 Purpose

This register lists the most important regulations that apply to our plant and products, who is responsible and what they mean for us. It is an internal summary and does not replace the legal text. In case of doubt, the official text published in the Official Journal of the EU or the German Federal Law Gazette applies.

## 2 Machinery and work equipment

| Regulation | What it means for us | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC | Applies to machines we buy or build until 19 January 2027. CE marking and declaration of conformity required for new machines. | Production Engineering |
| Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 | Replaces the Machinery Directive and applies from 20 January 2027. Machines placed on the market from that date must follow the new regulation. Digital instructions are allowed under conditions. | Production Engineering |
| Work Equipment Directive 2009/104/EC and BetrSichV | Risk assessment and regular inspection of work equipment by qualified persons. | Maintenance |

## 3 Products

| Regulation | What it means for us | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| REACH Regulation (EC) 1907/2006 | We must know if our products contain substances of very high concern (SVHC) above 0.1 % by weight and inform customers. Suppliers report via IMDS. | Product Compliance |
| RoHS Directive 2011/65/EU | Not directly applicable to our vehicle e-drives, but several customers require RoHS conformity in their contracts. | Product Compliance |
| End-of-Life Vehicles Directive 2000/53/EC | Restricts lead, mercury, cadmium and hexavalent chromium in vehicle parts, with exemptions in Annex II. | Product Compliance |

## 4 Workplace safety and environment

| Regulation | What it means for us | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Noise Directive 2003/10/EC | See HSE-POL-007. | HSE |
| Hazardous Substances Ordinance (GefStoffV) | Workplace instructions and substitution check for hazardous substances. See HSE-POL-003. | HSE |
| ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 | Certified management systems for environment and occupational safety. Recertification audit due in 2027. | HSE |

## 5 Quality

| Standard | What it means for us | Owner |
|---|---|---|
