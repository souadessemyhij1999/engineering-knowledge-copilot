## 1 Audit data

Supplier: Kessler Gusstechnik, plant 2. Product: gearbox housing castings GH-210 (aluminium high pressure die casting). Audit type: process audit according to VDA 6.3. Audit date: 2026-05-12 and 2026-05-13.

## 2 Result

Overall degree of fulfilment: 78 %. Classification: B (conditionally approved). Two process elements were rated below 80 %:

| Process element | Result |
|---|---|
| P5 Supplier management | 82 % |
| P6.2 Process control during series production | 71 % |
| P6.4 Measurement and test equipment | 74 % |
| P7 Customer care | 88 % |

## 3 Main findings

1. Porosity check by X-ray is done on 1 part per shift only, while the control plan requires 1 part every 2 hours.
2. Two gauges used for the sealing face flatness were overdue for calibration by 3 months.
3. Reaction plan for vacuum system failure at die casting cell 4 is missing.

## 4 Required actions

The supplier must send an action plan within 10 working days. A follow-up audit is scheduled for November 2026. Until then, incoming inspection of GH-210 castings is increased to 5 parts per batch with X-ray. If the follow-up audit result is below 80 %, the supplier is put on new business hold.
