## 1 Purpose

This procedure makes sure that nonconforming product is identified, segregated and not used or delivered by mistake.

## 2 Identification and segregation

Whoever finds a nonconforming part must:

1. Attach a red tag with date, part number, quantity and a short description of the defect.
2. Put the part in the red box at the workstation or in quarantine cage Q1.
3. Inform the shift leader.

## 3 Nonconformity report (NCR)

The shift leader or quality creates an NCR in the quality system within the same shift. The NCR contains part number, serial or batch numbers, defect description, photo and quantity.

## 4 Decision

Only quality may decide on the disposition of nonconforming product:

| Disposition | Who decides |
|---|---|
| Rework according to an approved rework instruction | quality |
| Use as is (concession) | quality and engineering, customer approval if the part is delivered |
| Scrap | quality |
| Return to supplier | quality with purchasing |

## 5 Escalation

If the same defect occurs 3 times within one week or the defect can affect safety or the customer, quality starts an 8D problem solving process.
