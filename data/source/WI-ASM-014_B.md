## 1 Purpose

This work instruction describes how the gearbox housing cover is sealed and bolted to the housing on assembly line L2. It applies to the e-drive variants ED-150 and ED-210.

## 2 Safety

Wear cut-resistant gloves when handling the housing cover. Wear nitrile gloves when working with the sealant. The EC screwdriver may only be used with the reaction arm fitted.

## 3 Tightening torques

| Joint | Thread | Torque | Tolerance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Housing cover bolts | M8 | 25 Nm | +/- 2.5 Nm |
| Flange bolts to motor housing | M10 | 40 Nm | +/- 4 Nm |
| Speed sensor bracket | M6 | 9 Nm | +/- 0.9 Nm |
| Oil drain plug | M14x1.5 | 30 Nm | +/- 3 Nm |

## 4 Procedure

1. Check the EC screwdriver with the torque analyser at the start of every shift.
2. Clean the flange surfaces with a lint-free cloth and cleaning agent C-1.
3. Apply a continuous sealant bead of 2.5 mm +/- 0.5 mm.
4. Fit the cover within 15 minutes of applying the sealant.
5. Tighten the bolts in a cross pattern in two stages: 50 % and 100 % of the final torque.
6. Let the sealant cure for at least 20 minutes before the leak test.
