## 1 Summary

September was a stable month for assembly, but machining had the bearing seat problem on MC-504 (8D-2026-021). The internal scrap rate went up mainly because of this.

## 2 Key figures

| KPI | Target | August | September |

| Open 8D reports | - | 2 | 2 |

## 3 Top defects in September

| Defect | Quantity | Area |
|---|---|---|
| Bearing seat diameter out of tolerance | 38 | Machining MC-504 |
| Insulation resistance too low at EOL test | 14 | EOL test |
| Paint damage on housing | 11 | Logistics |
| Connector pin bent | 7 | Electronic pre-assembly |

## 4 Comments

The insulation resistance failures were caused by moisture in stator windings from one supplier batch. The batch was blocked. The leak test failures dropped to zero after the changes from 8D-2026-017. Supplier ppm is still above target, mainly because of porosity in housing castings from Kessler Gusstechnik.
