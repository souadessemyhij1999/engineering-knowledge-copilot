## 1 Reason for change

Customer complaint 8D-2026-017 (oil leakage at the gearbox flange). The joint analysis showed that the clamping force at 40 Nm was too low after thermal cycling for the sealant bead in use.

## 2 Description of change

| Item | Old | New |
|---|---|---|
| Torque M10 flange bolts | 40 Nm +/- 4 Nm | 45 Nm +/- 4.5 Nm |
| Sealant bead width | 2.5 mm | 3.0 mm |
| Sealant open time | 15 min | 10 min |
| Curing time before leak test | 20 min | 30 min |
| Bead inspection | visual by operator | camera inspection plus operator confirmation |

## 3 Validation

The 45 Nm torque was validated by the bolt calculation according to VDI 2230 and by 500 units with thermal cycling (-40 to +120 degrees C, 50 cycles). The M10 bolt strength class 10.9 is not changed. Surface pressure under the bolt head stays below the limit of the aluminium housing.

## 4 Affected documents

WI-ASM-014 (Rev B -> C), WI-PNT-005 (Rev C -> D), PFMEA dispensing, control plan L2.

## 5 Effectivity

Effective from 2026-06-01, starting with serial number ED210-2606-00001. Units built before this date with 40 Nm do not need to be reworked if they passed the helium sniff test during containment.

## 6 Approval

Approved by product engineering, quality and production on 2026-05-27. Customer informed and approved the change on 2026-05-29.
