## 1 Purpose

This procedure defines how purchased parts are inspected at goods receipt and how the inspection effort is adjusted to the supplier performance.

## 2 Inspection levels

| Level | When | Sample |
|---|---|---|
| Normal | default | sampling plan ISO 2859-1, level II, AQL 0.65 |
| Tightened | after a rejected lot or supplier rated C | sampling plan level II, AQL 0.4 |
| Reduced | 10 lots in a row accepted and supplier rated A | sampling plan level I, AQL 0.65 |
| Skip lot | 20 lots in a row accepted, supplier rated A, safety parts excluded | every 5th lot |

Safety-relevant parts (marked with the S symbol in the drawing) are never on skip lot.

## 3 First article inspection

For every new part or after a change at the supplier, a first article inspection (FAI) with full dimensional report and material certificate 3.1 is required before the first series delivery.

## 4 Rejected lots

A rejected lot is labelled with a red label and moved to quarantine cage Q1. A nonconformity report is created according to QM-NCR-001 and the supplier is informed within 24 hours.

## 5 Supplier rating

Suppliers are rated every quarter on quality (ppm), delivery and reaction to complaints. A: 90 points or more, B: 75 to 89 points, C: below 75 points.
