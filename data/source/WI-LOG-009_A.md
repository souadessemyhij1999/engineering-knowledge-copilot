## 1 Purpose

This instruction describes how material is supplied to the assembly lines and how it is labelled and stored so that the oldest material is used first (first in, first out).

## 2 Labelling

Every box at the line must carry a label with part number, batch number, quantity and goods receipt date. Boxes without a readable label must not be used. Bring them back to the logistics office.

## 3 FIFO

- Always take material from the front of the flow rack. New boxes are loaded from the back.
- Partly used boxes go back to the front position, not on top of new boxes.
- Material older than 6 months must be released again by quality before use.

## 4 Storage

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Maximum stack height of KLT boxes | 3 boxes |
| Minimum aisle width | 1.2 m |
| Quarantine storage | Locked cage Q1 next to goods receipt |
| Quarantine label | Red label |

Material with a red label may only be moved by quality.

## 5 Forklifts and tuggers

Only trained and authorised drivers may operate forklifts and tugger trains. The speed limit in the production halls is 6 km/h.
